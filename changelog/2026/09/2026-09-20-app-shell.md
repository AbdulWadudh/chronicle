# App shell: sign-in, avatar menu, world scope

**Date:** 2026-09-20
**Scope:** apps/stage

## What changed

- Sign-in collapsed from a two-column split to a single centred column at every width.
- The Worlds header's three loose buttons became an avatar that opens an account menu — identity,
  Settings, Admin (admins only) and Sign out.
- World cards and the scope control gained the shared press and stagger treatment.

## Why

At 1440px the sign-in split gave the brand column `flex-1` inside a 1040px container, so a 52px
logo and one line of text sat stranded roughly 500px from the form, with the pair floating in
dead space. A single centred column is the standard shape for the screen and works at 375px and
1440px without a breakpoint branch — it also deleted the `isSplit` branching and the
`useResponsiveLayout` dependency.

The scope tabs were bare `<button role="tab">` elements styled with nothing but a background
colour, so they never picked up border width, radius or shadow. They looked unthemed next to
everything else because they were. Replaced with the `Segmented` primitive, which reports
`borderWidth: 1px, borderRadius: 12px` from tokens.

## Evidence

- Sign-in captured at 375px and 1440px; content centred with no stranded column.
- Scope control computed styles read from tokens after the swap, rather than the flat
  `bg-primary` / `bg-secondary` it had before.

## Follow-ups

- `Play`, `CharacterSelect` and `Composer` have not been checked for the same opt-out.
- The profile screen for name, password and avatar is not built; only its endpoints exist.
